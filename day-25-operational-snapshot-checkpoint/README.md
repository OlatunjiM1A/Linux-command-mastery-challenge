## What I practiced
CHECKPOINT. I built a one-screen operational snapshot of my system
covering uptime, memory usage, the status of three key services, and
any scheduled cron jobs.

## What surprised me
Pulling uptime, memory, service status, and cron jobs together into one
combined view made it obvious how these commands complement each other
in real troubleshooting — none of them alone tells the full story, but
together they give a fast "is this system healthy right now" read
that would take much longer to piece together one command at a time.
