See [commands.md](commands.md) for the complete list and my explanations.

## What I Practiced

I worked with a Linux log file and used different commands to view its contents, inspect specific sections, count its lines, identify its file type, and examine its metadata.

## What Surprised Me

The commands I thought were just for "reading files" turn out to also be early-warning tools in log analysis. Stat catches tampered timestamps, wc -l flags abnormal log spikes, and tail -f shows an attack happening live.
